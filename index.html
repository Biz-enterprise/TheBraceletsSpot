Good to Know

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Join the WhatsApp group or message Aniebiet directly using the button below. Share the style, colors, and (for custom pieces) the name or names you'd like included.

How long does a custom piece take?

Most bracelets are hand-knotted within a few days of confirming your order. Larger sets (squad or family) may take a little longer since each piece is made individually.

Can I mix styles within a set?

Yes — couple, bestie, squad, sibling, and family sets can mix colors or styles per person while keeping a shared theme. Just mention this when you order.

Do you deliver?

Message Aniebiet directly with your location to confirm delivery options and pricing.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared directly by Aniebiet once your order and pricing are confirmed — just ask in chat.
